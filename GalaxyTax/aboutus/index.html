Our Mission

At Galaxy Tax, we believe that financial services should be clear, accessible, and tailored to the needs of real people. We are a bilingual tax and bookkeeping business dedicated to supporting individuals, families, and small businesses with reliable, year-round financial guidance.
Our mission is simple:
To provide honest, accurate, and affordable financial services that empower our community to thrive. We understand that taxes and bookkeeping can feel overwhelming. That’s why we focus on clear communication, personalized attention, and dependable support in both English and Spanish.

Who We Serve

We proudly work with:

  • Individuals and families
  • Small business owners
  • Self-employed and independent contractors
  • New entrepreneurs starting their first business
  • Community members who prefer service in Spanish

Whether you need help filing taxes, managing payroll, or staying organized financially, we are here to make the process smooth and stress-free.

Our Values

✔ Integrity — Honest, transparent service every step of the way
